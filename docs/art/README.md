## cheerful-face-sketch.png

**By Kellan N.** Included with permission.

A quick reference sketch of how a cheerful chibi face should read, drawn to guide the 3D characters in
BeachBlox. What it shows:

- **A big shine** in the upper-left of each eye: one large oval, on the same side in both eyes, as if lit by a
  single light.
- **Happy eyes:** tall eyes whose lower edge is pushed up and flattened, as if by the cheeks.
- **Big blush cheeks** that overlap the lower, outer edge of each eye. A smile that reaches the eyes is what
  makes a face read as warm rather than mechanical.
- **An open grin:** flat on top, rounded underneath, corners curling up.

The artist notes it's deliberately a little extra cheerful to make the idea clear.

Kellan N. gave permission (2026-10-15) for this sketch to be included and released with this repository. Her sketches are
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (confirmed 2026-10-15).

## cheerful-face-sketch-2.png

**By Kellan N.** Included with permission (2026-10-15, as above).

A cleaner, more polished version of the same reference. It shows the same ideas more precisely: tall eyes
with one large shine on the same side of each, a small second sparkle near the bottom, the lower edge of each
eye lifted and flattened by the cheeks, blush ovals overlapping the lower outer edge of the eyes, and an open
grin that's flat on top and rounded underneath. Kellan N. also suggested varied fringes per hairstyle
(not every fringe spiky), which the avatars now follow.
