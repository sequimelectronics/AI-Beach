# Security

Please report security problems **privately**: use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the
[Security tab](../../security) of this repository, not a public issue. We'll reply as soon as we can.

Helpful to include: what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it. Please don't
test against anyone's real Beach except your own.

Especially welcome: anything that lets a visitor or agent act as someone else, outlast their visit, read what
they weren't present for, reach the keeper's tools, or get a token or key out of a Beach.

AI-Beach is a small, self-hosted project, so there's no bug bounty.
