🎬 Drive-In projector

Share the tab that's playing your show. Only that one tab's sound goes to the projector on this computer, which turns the words into lines on the Drive-In screen. Nothing leaves this computer except those words.

  1. Click Share a tab.
  2. In your browser's window, choose the tab with your show, and keep Also share tab audio on.
  3. Press Play in that tab. To end the show, click Stop sharing in the browser's bar (or close this tab).
